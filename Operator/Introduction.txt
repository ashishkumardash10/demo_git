						OPERATOR
						========
1- What is operator ?
ans - In MySQL, operators are symbols or keywords used to perform operations on data. They are commonly used in SELECT, WHERE, UPDATE, DELETE, and other SQL statements.

- There are 7 main types of operators in MySQL.

No.	Operator Type	Purpose
1	Arithmetic Operators	Perform mathematical calculations.
2	Comparison (Relational) Operators	Compare two values.
3	Logical Operators	Combine multiple conditions.
4	Special Operators	Perform special comparisons and searches.
6	Assignment Operator	Assign a value to a user-defined variable.





Arithmetic Operator
-------------------

- Arithmetic operators are used to perform mathematical calculations on numeric values.
	ex - + ,-,*,/,%

	-" + "
		SELECT salary + 5000 AS new_salary
		FROM Employee;

	-"-"     SELECT salary - 2000 AS salary_after_deduction
		FROM Employee;
	
	-"*"     SELECT salary * 12 AS annual_salary
		FROM Employee;

	-"/"    SELECT *FROM Employee
		WHERE salary / 12 > 3000;

	-"%"	SELECT salary % 1000 AS remainder
		FROM Employee;



What are Relational Operators?

- Relational operators are used to compare two values. The result of the comparison is either TRUE or FALSE.	
		
		<,>,<=,>=,!= or <> ,=
	
	1. Equal To (=)
	Find employees whose salary is 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary = 30000;



	2. Not Equal To (!=)
	Find employees whose salary is not 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary != 30000;


	3. Not Equal To (<>)
	Find The Employee Which Salary Is Not Equals To 3000
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary <> 30000;

	4. Greater Than (>)
	Find employees with salary greater than 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary > 30000;

	
	5. Less Than (<)
	Find employees with salary less than 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary < 30000;
	
	6 .Greater Than or Equal To (>=)
	Find employees with salary greater than or equal to 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary >= 30000;

	7. Less Than or Equal To (<=)
	Find employees with salary less than or equal to 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	WHERE salary <= 30000;



What Is Logical Operators in MySQL ?
 -Logical operators are used to combine two or more conditions in a SQL query. They always return TRUE or FALSE.
		ex -AND ,OR ,NOT

		
AND – Returns rows only when all conditions are TRUE.
OR – Returns rows when at least one condition is TRUE.
NOT – Reverses the result of a condition.


	AND
	---
	Condition: Find employees who are in the IT department and have a salary greater than 30000.
	SELECT *FROM Employee
	HERE department = 'IT' AND salary > 30000;


	OR
	--
 Find employees who are in the IT department or have a salary greater than 45000.
SELECT *FROM Employee
WHERE department = 'IT' OR salary > 45000;



	. NOT
	------
Condition: Find employees who are not in the IT department.
SELECT *FROM Employee
WHERE NOT department = 'IT';
